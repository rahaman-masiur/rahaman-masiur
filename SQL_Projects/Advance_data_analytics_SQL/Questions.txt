KEY ANALYSIS QUESTIONS
======================
A list of the core business questions this project sets out to answer, organized by analysis type.

DATABASE SETUP & DATA CLEANING (table_and_dataset.sql)
--------------------------------------------------------
1. Are there any sales records that reference a customer that doesn't exist in the customer table?
2. Are there any products with missing/blank category values that should be removed?
3. What years of order data are present in the sales table, and can the table be partitioned by year for better performance?

TREND ANALYSIS / CHANGE OVER TIME (Trend_analysis.sql)
--------------------------------------------------------
4. What are the total sales for each individual day?
5. What are the total sales for each month (across all years)?
6. What are total sales for each quarter of each year?
7. What is the total yearly revenue?
8. What is the total quantity of products sold each month?
9. How many distinct customers placed an order each month? Each year?
10. How many total orders were placed each quarter, and what is the running yearly order total?

CUMULATIVE ANALYSIS (Cumulative_analysis.sql)
--------------------------------------------------------
11. What is the running (cumulative) total of sales over time, month by month?
12. What is the moving average of product price over time?
13. What is the cumulative total quantity sold over time, month by month?

PERFORMANCE ANALYSIS (Performance_analysis.sql)
--------------------------------------------------------
14. How does each product's yearly revenue compare to its own average revenue (above/below average)?
15. How does each product's yearly revenue compare to its previous year's revenue (year-over-year growth or decline)?
16. How does each month's revenue compare to the average revenue for that year?
17. How does each month's revenue compare to the previous month's revenue (month-over-month growth or decline)?
18. What is the average order value for customers in each country?

PART-TO-WHOLE ANALYSIS (Part_to_whole_analysis.sql)
--------------------------------------------------------
19. Which product categories contribute the most to overall revenue, and what is their percentage share?
20. Which individual products contribute the most to overall revenue, and what is their percentage share?
21. Which countries generate the most revenue, and what is their percentage share of total revenue?
22. Which customer age groups contribute the most to total revenue, and what is their percentage share?

DATA SEGMENTATION (Data_segmentation.sql)
--------------------------------------------------------
23. How many customers fall into each age group (≤20, 21–40, 41–60, 60+)?
24. How many products fall into each cost range (below 500, 500–1000, 1000–1500, above 1500), and what is the average cost per range?
25. How can customers be segmented into VIP, Regular, and New categories based on how long they've been ordering and how much they've spent?
26. How many customers fall into each of the VIP / Regular / New segments?

CUSTOMER REPORT (Report.sql)
--------------------------------------------------------
27. What does a consolidated view of each customer's order history look like (product, sales amount, quantity, age)?
28. For each customer, what is their most recent order date, total amount spent, total quantity purchased, and total distinct products purchased?
29. What is each customer's order lifespan (in months) and average monthly spending?
30. What is each customer's average order value?
31. Who are the top customers when ranked by total spending?
